Takeaway
Discipline on entry & exit beats a cushion — and idle capital isn't idle. The best risk-adjusted book is VIX≥15 + 40% PT + premium-2.5× blow-up stop + DTE-5 (Calmar ~1). Layering the BankNifty cushion lifts Calmar (0.98→1.21 with liquid yield) and cuts the drawdown ~26% — worth it for the best risk-adjusted book. With the 6% liquid yield on, the VIX gate's flat months earn interest — so gating costs far less return than it first appears while keeping the drawdown low.
