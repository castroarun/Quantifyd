What shipped, and the rule that keeps it true

A and C are both live at /app/strategies, behind a Register / Spec switch in the page header (the choice sticks per browser). Clicking a system name — or its Rules chip — in the Register jumps straight to that system's Spec. The boxed metric tiles are gone: the Spec pane now opens on an unboxed stat line with hairline dividers, as shown in Mock C above. B stayed a mock.

Every row carries three link slots, and a missing one is a visible gap rather than a silent omission: Rules (the STATUS/design doc), Study (/app/backtest/<slug>, with its verdict), Dashboard (the live page). "Study pending" in grey is a to-do you can see from across the room.

Proposed addition to .claude/CLAUDE.md — THE STRATEGIES INDEX IS THE REGISTER OF RECORD. Whenever a system goes live, goes to paper, is parked, changes size, or has a rule changed, the same commit updates the Strategies index — status, size, the rule line, and a dated change-log entry. A rule change that is not in the index is not deployed.

The register ships with the split resolved from the repo: Momentum ₹3L = live (real money, moved under Holdings), ORB Cash = paper (resumed in paper mode with a paper-aware margin gate), KC6 = parked (scheduler runs, strategy not being pursued). Day P&L is shown only where a live feed is already wired — ORB Cash, ORB Index, NAS NIFTY, NAS SENSEX — and reads "—" elsewhere rather than inventing a number.
