## The concrete sleeve spec (if Arun adopts)

  29.6/−14.9/2.02 without it. Give up ~1.3pp CAGR for ~3pp less drawdown and improvement in

- **Review clause (recommend registering in Ops):** re-examine the sleeve if gold draws down
  >25% from entry or after any 12-month period where the blend Calmar delta vs no-gold turns
  negative — the edge is diversification, not a gold-bull call, and the window is
  gold-favorable.

## Honest caveats

1. **Gold-regime dependence — the big one.** 2015-2026 contains two major gold bull runs;
   gold's real long-run return is ~0-2% with 20-30% drawdowns (2013 −25%-type years are
   OUTSIDE our data). The Calmar lift is partly regime. This is why w3=10% (minimum passing)
   is the recommendation and a review clause is attached. The DIVERSIFICATION argument
   (corr ≈ 0, crash-positive, grind-positive) is structural; the RETURN contribution is not.
2. **2008 untestable** for all candidates (data starts 2015); gold's 2008 behavior globally
   was favorable (+ in INR terms), but that is prior knowledge, not our backtest.
3. Candidate sleeves gross-of-tax by design (kill-safe asymmetry); the B&H gold sleeve's
   deferral is genuine, so the passing result is not tax-flattered in any material way.
4. Blend-level monthly rebalancing tax between sleeves is ignored — consistently with how
   the TN+OA baseline itself is computed (the comparison is fair; absolute levels optimistic).
5. GILT/GNG3 numbers carry thin-ETF dislocation prints (Mar-2020 ±16-19% days).
6. Multiple testing: 12 constructions × 5 weights tried; guarded by the pre-registered bar,
   the same-window re-anchored baseline, the cash-null dominance requirement, offset/seed
   robustness, and the monotone (not cherry-picked) weight response.

## What was NOT tested, and why

- 2008 behavior of any candidate (no data before 2015 for the ETFs — stated).
- International gold spot / longer gold history via other sources (out of DB; would
  strengthen or weaken the regime caveat — worth a data pull if Arun considers adoption).
- SGBs (sovereign gold bonds — better carry than GOLDBEES but illiquid/lumpy; implementation
  detail for adoption, not backtest).
- Silver (SILVERBEES 2022+ only), REITs/InvITs (2019+, thin), international ex-Nasdaq.
- Futures-based gold (MCX) — no data, and ETF suffices at this size.
- Deeper trend-filter grids on gold/Nasdaq — the simple filter already lost in-window;
  tuning it would be regime-fitting.
