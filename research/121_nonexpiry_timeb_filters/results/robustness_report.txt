  mean(exc5 - exc1) = 0.0000 bp   max|diff| = 0.0000 bp   rows differing = 0
  start-price mismatch rows = 0
  => the maximum excursion inside a fixed window is RESOLUTION-INVARIANT
     (a window's high is the max of the bar highs however you bucket them).
     The no-5-min rule bites on the PATH - which minute a stop fires - and that
     part of this study is done on the real 1-minute option chain, not on bars.
