## 2. The bridge - and the point at which the LINEAR bridge becomes unsafe
B1 linear (r/122 method): b_median = 0.01084 frac/bp on n=13 days with exc>=20bp
   dimensionless: F_lin(R) = 0.331 x R   (R = index distance / credit)
   tail variant (median over the 4 largest excursions): F_lin_t(R) = 0.413 x R
B2 intrinsic floor (model-free): F_intr(R) = max(0, R - 1)
   -> the two CROSS at R = 1/(1-0.331) = 1.49. Below that the fitted slope is the
      better description; ABOVE it the linear bridge understates the loss and the
      intrinsic floor is the only safe one. THIS is r/122's 'bridged tails are
      FLOORS' warning, made quantitative: at R=3 the linear bridge says the straddle
      is 0.99 credits under water; arithmetic says it is at least 2.00.
B3 = the observed worst on the recorded sample.
F(R) = max(F_lin, F_intr) is what is used from here on.
