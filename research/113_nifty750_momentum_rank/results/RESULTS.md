construction. The honest test is the point-in-time walk-forward described below.

## Why this is a screen and not a finding

1. **Survivorship in the constituent list is severe and unavoidable here.** Membership of Nifty 750
   TODAY is partly *caused* by having rallied. Ranking today's members by trailing 12-month return
   therefore selects on the outcome. CUPID's +722% is the clearest case: it is in the index and at
   the top of this list for the same reason. research/87+88 killed two earlier screens for exactly
   this, which is why drift + date-matched + random-entry controls are mandatory before any claim.
2. **Nothing here is backtested.** Whether a 750 universe beats the live Nifty 200 for THIS book is
   untested. research/106 tested Nifty 500 and found churn rises with universe width; 750 would be
   worse on that axis, and churn is a real cost (brokerage + 20% STCG).
3. **A very high rsblend often means the move already happened.** A 5.95 is roughly six times the
   index; momentum rules buy strength, but the tail of this distribution is where blow-off tops
   live. The live book's top-22 buffer exists precisely to avoid chasing single-name extremes.
4. Today's bar is a LIVE partial bar (run at 10:48 with the market open), so the last day moves.

## What would make this a finding

Re-rank on a POINT-IN-TIME constituent list (membership as it was, not as it is), then walk it
forward with the book's real rules — rebalance cadence, top-22 buffer, Donchian stop, macro gate —
and compare net-of-cost against the live Nifty 200 book over the same window. Until then this
answers "what does the rule point at today", nothing more.
