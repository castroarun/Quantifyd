How the spec evolved — the questions that changed the rules

Every rule below started as a question, got a test, and only then entered (or left) the spec. Current spec: NSR-W v1.4 — Monday ~15:00 entry · next-week expiry · ENTER at ₹30/leg · GTT stop 2.0×/leg · PT 50% of credit · first stop → close both & RE-STRANGLE, and EOD skew ≥1.5× → recenter — both adjustments at ₹20/leg (once each per week; t 6.9) · second stop closes that leg only, survivor rides · out by DTE-1 · fixed lots · margin ≤ 70%. (Note: the travel charts below were replayed under v1.1 roll rules; the nightly regen carries the live rules forward for live-paper weeks.)

1 · Entry time: 09:30 was an untested guess — the data says ~15:00

The first replay used 09:30 by convention ("let the open settle") — never backtested. Challenged ("why not 09:16/09:20?"), an 8-point entry-time sweep ran on the 13 recorded weeks: a smooth gradient, later = better — ₹30 at 09:16 → 17.0 pts/wk (t 2.4), 09:30 → 16.7 (t 2.2), 09:45 → 21.1 (t 3.9), 11:00 → 21.9 (t 4.3), 15:14 → 24.2 (t 4.5). Decisively, the 378-week EOD study prices at daily closes — it was always implicitly a Monday-close-entry system, so the big-sample t 5.5 belongs to the late entry. Mechanism: at 8–9 DTE, waiting until 15:00 costs almost no theta but sets strikes AFTER Monday's move is known and skips six hours of day-one gamma.

2 · Profit-take: 40–50% is the plateau; deeper is worse

"Why 50%, not 60/70?" → swept 40/50/60/70/80/none on all 378 EOD weeks (₹30, stop 2.0×): PT40 t 5.48 and PT50 t 5.47 tie at the top; PT60–80 and no-PT drop to t 3.8–4.3 with no mean gain. The 13-week replay had hinted PT80 — the hint died on the big sample (small-sample mirage; every replay hint is re-tested on 378 weeks before touching the spec).

3 · After a stop: RE-STRANGLE (v1.3) — the user's second winning idea

v1.2 rolled only the stopped side at the ₹-target, survivor riding. Question raised: "if the survivor is worth ₹6, matching its premium gives nothing — should we close BOTH and sell fresh on both sides?" Tested four roll styles on 378 weeks (₹30 base): fixed one-side 11.73 (t 5.84) · match-survivor 10.74 (t 5.74) · halfway-₹15 10.96 (t 5.73) · close-both-and-re-strangle 12.93 — t 6.76, the strongest result of the entire program, better on mean, tail (worst −407 vs −422), p5 (−34.7) and post-22 (+15.9). So v1.3: the FIRST stop closes the whole book and re-sells a fresh symmetric ₹-target pair (once per cycle); a second stop closes that leg only, survivor rides. Unifying principle with the EOD recenter: when the book breaks, don't patch a leg — re-run the entry.
v1.4 refinement (also user-raised): "a fresh ₹30 at 15:15 sits closer to the market" — correct. Adjustment-target sweep (₹30/24/20/15 on the ₹30 book): ₹20–24 is the plateau (t 6.92–6.94, p5 −30 vs −34.7), ₹15 too timid (t 5.75). Adopted: enter at ₹30, adjust (re-strangle & recenter) at ₹20. The ₹20-entry book keeps ₹20 adjustments — already far OTM, backing off further only costs credit.

4 · Overnight skew: exit-the-heavy-leg tested, then beaten by the user's recenter idea

Worry: "if CE/PE premiums are unbalanced at the close, aren't we exposed to a gap on the heavy side?" Test 1 — EOD guard (close the heavy leg at 15:15 if ≥1.5× credit): free insurance — t 5.47→5.63, mean unchanged. Test 2 — the user's counter-proposal: recenter — close the whole skewed book at 15:15 and re-sell a fresh equidistant-by-premium strangle: t 5.84 (once/week) to 5.92 — the strongest cell in the entire program, +0.7 pts/wk, tail unchanged. Why this is NOT the manual-rolling leak: it fires once, at the close, on a defined trigger, and restarts a symmetric book at the same risk distance — it restarts the system rather than defending a broken position. Adopted at once/week (v1.2). Honest limit: the worst weeks (−422/−445 pts) are unchanged by ANY guard — those gaps launched from unremarkable closes. The true tail defense remains sizing and margin headroom.

5 · Daily 09:16 in-and-out variant: tested and shelved

Selling a ₹10–30 premium strangle every morning at 09:16, same exits, flat by 15:15 — 64 days, 318 cycles: Wed/Thu/Fri net ≈ 0 after costs at every target; the entire edge sits on Monday (DTE 1) and Tuesday (expiry day), ~+8 pts/day at ₹30 — independently reproducing the research/51 finding that intraday premium edge lives on 0/1-DTE. Shelved: a Mon/Tue-only intraday book would stack the same short-gamma risk on top of NSR-W's Monday entry.

6 · What no rule fixed

Gap-and-grind weeks (worst EOD week −445 pts ≈ −₹2.9L at 10 lots, Feb 2026) survive every stop, guard and recenter variant tested. The mitigations are structural: fixed size you can survive, margin utilization ≤ 70%, and a proposed (untested) event-skip rule for election/budget/Fed weeks.

13-week execution validation on real recorded quotes (entries at bid, exits at ask — spreads and GTT slippage are real, including a 13.7-pt slip in the Jul 8 crash minute). This window was a friendly regime (VIX 12–15); the long-run expectation comes from the 378-week EOD study (t 4.8–5.5), not from these weeks. Charges model: 0.25% of premium + 0.1 pt per cycle.
